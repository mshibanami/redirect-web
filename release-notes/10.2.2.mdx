- **Browser Extensions**
  - Fixed an issue where DNR rules stopped working after modifying another rule.
    - For example, suppose there are 2 enabled DNR rules, A and B. When launching the browser, they both work as expected. However, once A is disabled or its title is modified, B also stops working until the browser is restarted.
    - This was caused by an issue in RedirectWeb's internal rule update logic. We apologize for the inconvenience.
- **App**
  - Improved the dark mode UI visibility.
  - Fixed a typo in the Paywall screen.

The DNR issue and the typo were both reported by users. Thank you very much for your cooperation! 🙇
